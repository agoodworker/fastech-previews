About Lapeau Medi Spa

A nurse-led Malvern clinic, established 2018

Lapeau Medi Spa is a non-surgical skin clinic at 19 Glenferrie Rd, Malvern. Its team includes registered nurses with 10+ and 13+ years' experience cited on the clinic's site, Alma-certified practitioners and ADNA members, with a dermatopathologist advisor.

The clinic is led by director Sue Lee, whose September 2026 journal post describes its personalised medispa approach: treatment planned around the individual, not sold off a shelf. No other founding history is claimed here beyond what the clinic itself publishes.
