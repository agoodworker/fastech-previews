## How a preview works

1. We research the business: its services, prices, reviews and customers.
2. We design a complete homepage concept using that real information.
3. The owner reviews the concept live, in the browser, with no obligation.
4. On approval, we build the full website on the business's own domain and hosting.

## What we do

Fastech Digital Solutions designs and builds business websites, redesigns, online booking pages and advertising landing pages, together with SEO, care plans, custom software and AI integrations. Our approach is simple: we show the finished concept first, so a business never has to buy a website it has not seen.
