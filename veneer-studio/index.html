About The Veneer Studio

A specialist practice, not a general clinic

The Veneer Studio is a cosmetic dentistry practice focused on veneers and digital smile design. Rather than operating a standalone front door, it works out of two established Melbourne clinics: MODA Dental in Malvern East and Dentology Dental Care in Aspendale.

Its published treatments span porcelain veneers, composite bonding and veneers, Invisalign, crowns, implants, teeth whitening, smile makeovers, digital smile design, gummy smile treatment and gum contouring. A free consultation, valued at $299, is the published starting point, and flexible payment plans are offered. No founding year or clinician biographies are published in the sources used for this page, so none are claimed here.
