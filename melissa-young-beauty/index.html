05 — About

A Glebe beauty studio since 2014

Melissa Young Beauty is a beauty and cosmetic brow-tattoo studio at Shop 2, 197 Glebe Point Road, Glebe, Sydney. It has traded since 2014, and its core work is cosmetic brow tattooing — microblading, ombré shading and combination brows — alongside brow shaping, lamination, lashes, skin treatments and bridal services.

Its published approach is precise and upfront: every tattoo option includes mapping and shaping, one treatment and aftercare products, results are stated as lasting around 6–18 months, and touch-ups follow a published price ladder. Its product shop stocks DMK, Ultraceuticals, Mesoestetic and Omnilux LED. No individual team biographies are published in the sources used for this page, so none are claimed here.
