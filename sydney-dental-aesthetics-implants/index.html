The decision page, in one view

Titanium implants

The long-proven standard: strong, well documented, and the right choice for most implant cases including full-arch work.

  • Single, multiple and full-arch cases
  • All-on-4 concepts where appropriate
  • Established long-term track record

Zirconia implants — Straumann Pure

Metal-free ceramic implants for patients who want no metal in their mouth, or whose case suits a ceramic solution.

  • Metal-free, tooth-coloured material
  • Chosen case by case, not as a default
  • Assessed at consultation alongside titanium

Which material suits you is decided after examination — never sold from this page alone.
