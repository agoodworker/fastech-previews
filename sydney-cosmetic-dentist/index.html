What we do, in plain language

Questions patients actually ask

Is the $3,150 implant figure all-inclusive?

No — the published figure is "typically from $3,150 each" and its scope is not stated. Your consultation confirms exactly what is included for your case before you commit.

Can I book a single treatment online?

There is no per-treatment online booking calendar. Book through the form below, by phone on 02 5502 1236, or by email.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes — payment plans up to 24 months on eligible treatment.

What is a Perfect 10 smile makeover?

A planned combination of veneers and related cosmetic work, designed around your face and previewed with Digital Smile Design.
