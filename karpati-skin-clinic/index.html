Our story

Founded by Eva Karpati, in Double Bay.

Karpati Skin Clinic — Karpati Medispa — is a Double Bay medispa founded by Eva Karpati. Its heritage is in hands-on, results-driven skin care: facials, laser and body work delivered in a calm, personal clinic, where clients are known rather than processed.

This concept keeps that heritage and fixes the front door. Today, booking is explicitly “link in bio” and DM-led, with no native online booking and no per-treatment landing pages. The concept replaces that with proper booking and price-transparent treatment pages — the same warmth, with a booking system that works while the clinic is closed.
