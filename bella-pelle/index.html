Questions

Before you book.

Can I book online right now?

On the clinic's current site, booking is by form and a “Book a consultation” step — there is no live calendar. This concept adds consultation booking completable in three clicks.

Why are there no prices here?

Because the clinic does not publish a full price menu. Quoting an older third-party figure as a clinic price would be misleading, so none is quoted.

What are the clinic hours?

Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00. Saturday and Sunday hours are not published.

Is this the clinic's live website?

No. This is a concept homepage by Fastech Digital Solutions. Contact details shown are the clinic's own-site published details.
